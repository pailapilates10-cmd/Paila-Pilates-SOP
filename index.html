14

How updates should work

  1. Capture: collect current practice, owner intent, and source material.
  2. Draft: convert it into a structured SOP using the repository template.
  3. Review: confirm accuracy, responsibility, exceptions, and privacy.
  4. Publish: merge the approved change into the handbook.
  5. Maintain: update the change log and review when the process changes.
